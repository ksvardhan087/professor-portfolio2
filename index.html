About Dr. A. V. Paramkusam

Dedicated educator and researcher shaping the future of signal processing, visual computing, and outcome-based engineering education.

Dr. A. V. Paramkusam is a Professor of Electronics and Communication Engineering with over 26 years of extensive experience spanning classroom teaching, doctoral research, curriculum innovation, and high-level institutional administration.

His research primarily focuses on computationally efficient motion estimation algorithms for video coding standards, digital image and video watermarking, machine learning, deep learning architectures, and AI-assisted signal and medical image processing.

He has published extensively in peer-reviewed SCI-Expanded, Scopus, and Web of Science indexed journals and international conferences. Throughout his career, he has held vital administrative positions including Vice Principal (Academics), Head of Department, IIC Coordinator, and Smart India Hackathon SPOC.

Research Focus & Areas

Investigating efficient computational algorithms, modern video compression codecs, and artificial intelligence applications.

1. Motion Estimation in Video Coding

Design and evaluation of low-complexity fast search block-matching algorithms, multi-layer frame referencing, and transform-domain partial distortion matching for high-efficiency video coding (HEVC/H.265).

HEVC / H.265 Block Matching Multilayer Search

2. Digital Image & Video Watermarking

Robust embedding algorithms in DWT, DCT, and SVD domains designed for video copyright protection, broadcast monitoring, and tamper resistance in multimedia data.

DWT-SVD Copyright Protection Video Security

3. Machine Learning & Deep Learning

Application of deep neural networks, CNN backbones, and reinforcement learning for adaptive search window optimization and automated pattern recognition.

CNNs Deep Learning Pattern Recognition

4. AI-Based Signal Processing

Smart algorithms for adaptive noise filtering, wavelet transform decompositions, spectral estimation, and real-time signal analysis in communication networks.

Wavelet Transforms Spectral Analysis Adaptive Filters

5. Medical Image Processing

Computer-aided diagnostics, brain tumor and lesion detection in MRI/CT scans, and contrast optimization for tele-medicine and clinical decision support systems.

MRI / CT Analysis Tele-Healthcare Smart Medical

6. Image Segmentation

Advanced thresholding, region growing, and U-Net based semantic segmentation techniques for high-resolution satellite and biomedical visual data.

U-Net Architecture Semantic Segmentation

7. Lightweight Visual Intelligence Systems

Hardware-efficient, low-footprint visual models optimized for deployment on resource-constrained embedded platforms and real-time edge devices.

Edge AI Embedded Vision Low Power Computing
